SaraFin Privacy Policy

App: SaraFin (Family Finance Simplified) · Developer: Saralabs · Last updated: 15 November 2026

SaraFin is a family expense and finance app. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is kept, who can see it, and how you can delete it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

1. Information the app uses

InformationWhyWhere it is kept
Your name, email address and passwordTo create and sign in to your accountFirebase Authentication (Google). We never see or store your password ourselves.
Account details: user ID, family name and ID, your role (Admin or member), the relationship you choose (for example Spouse or Child), permissions set by the Admin, and your plan and trial datesTo run families, approvals and the free trialCloud Firestore (Google)
Transactions you enter: amount, date, type, category, sub-category, payment method, notes, who spent and for whomTo show your reports and budgets. They are uploaded to the cloud only when you press Sync and you belong to a family.On your phone always; in Cloud Firestore after you sync
Reminders, budgets, investments, settings and your PINTo provide those featuresOn your phone only. Your PIN is stored only as a salted hash and is never uploaded or included in backups.
App and device checks (App Check / Play Integrity)To confirm requests come from the genuine SaraFin app and to prevent abuseProcessed by Google

SaraFin does not use advertising, does not sell or rent your information, and does not include analytics or tracking tools.

2. Who can see your information

  • You see all of your own entries.
  • Your family. If you join a family, the family Admin can approve you, see your name and relationship, and decide what each member may view. A member sees other members' transactions only if the Admin allows it.
  • Google (Firebase) processes data on our behalf to store it and run sign-in. Their handling is described in Google's privacy policy and Firebase's data processing terms.
  • We do not share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

3. Offline use and storage

The app works without internet. Your entries are saved in a database on your phone. Information is sent over the internet only for sign-in, family features and when you press Sync. Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit, and Google encrypts stored data.

4. Permissions

  • Internet for sign-in and sync.
  • Notifications for bill and budget reminders you set. These are created on your phone.
  • Run after restart so your reminders keep working after the phone restarts.

5. Keeping and deleting your information

  • You can delete your account inside the app (Account & Privacy). This removes your sign-in account and your account details from the cloud and clears the data on your phone. If you are the Admin, the family and its cloud transactions are also removed.
  • If you are a member and delete your account, transactions you already synced may remain in your family's records for the Admin. Ask the Admin or write to us to have them removed.
  • You can also ask us to delete your information by emailing the address below. Please write from the email you registered with.
  • Entries you delete in the app move to the Recycle Bin and can be removed permanently by you or the Admin.

6. Free trial and plans

SaraFin currently offers a free trial. We store your trial dates in your account details. No payment information is collected at this time. If paid plans are added later, payments will be handled by Google Play, and this policy will be updated before that happens.

7. Children

SaraFin is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. A parent or guardian manages family accounts.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
